## Page architecture

The first fourteen contributions remain one physical page each. Nethanya is
marked as the featured best-friend contribution and expands into two ordinary
pages: a layered three-photo keepsake page and a sealed letter page. Because
fourteen pages precede it, those two pages naturally form a dedicated desktop
spread while remaining consecutive swipeable pages on mobile. This avoids any
changes to the page-turn engine.

The “Things We’ll Miss About Patty” list becomes its own physical page after
Nethanya. It pairs with the farewell page on desktop and remains a standalone
mobile page.

## Photo treatment

Recipes keep their handmade, non-repeating compositions. When a friend has more
photos than the recipe has visible frames, each frame becomes a tactile photo
stack. Tapping advances the stack while a small paper counter and offset edges
make the additional photos discoverable. Swiping from a photo still turns the
scrapbook page.

## Nethanya treatment

The left page reads as a private best-friend keepsake: all three supplied photos
overlap with tape, a handwritten “duo / best friend / sister / twinnie /
soulmate” note, and a 2022-to-forever thread. The right page holds a prominent
sealed letter that opens into the existing enlarged reading view so the full
long message is comfortable to read on both desktop and mobile.

## Missing content

Blank messages render as inert handmade paper rather than opening an empty
dialog. Placeholder photos remain obvious and easy to replace in the central
content object.
